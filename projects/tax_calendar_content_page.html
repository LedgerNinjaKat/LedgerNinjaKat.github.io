Overview

I'm excited to share a global tax compliance dashboard I built that automatically tracks payroll tax deadlines, statutory rates, and filing obligations across four major jurisdictions — Australia, the United Kingdom, Canada, and the United States. From federal withholding to state payroll taxes and provincial income brackets, everything updates in real time.

Many think managing multi-country payroll compliance requires an entire tax department, but the truth is that having a centralized, auto-updating calendar with official source links matters far more than scattered spreadsheets or manual tracking.

What Makes Compliance Tracking Work

The key skills for creating or using tax compliance dashboards effectively include:

📋

Map Filing Obligations

Organize every filing type by country and jurisdiction — BAS, STP, FPS, T4, Form 941, W-2, and dozens more — with due dates that auto-calculate from descriptive rules.

🗓️

Visualize Deadlines

Render monthly calendars with color-coded dots, upcoming deadline lists with urgency badges, and a hero countdown to the next critical filing.

🔗

Link Official Sources

Every filing card and statutory table includes direct links to ATO, HMRC, CRA, and IRS — so you verify before you file, every time.

Once you have these core skills, you can manage compliance across any number of jurisdictions without missing a deadline or relying on outdated rate sheets.

Customization Is Simple

Every business operates in different states, provinces, or territories. The dashboard handles this effortlessly — just select a country context and toggle between Federal / National and State / Provincial / Local views to see the exact obligations that apply to your operations.

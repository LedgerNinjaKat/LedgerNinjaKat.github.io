Overview

I'm excited to share a BAS preparation worksheet I built that can audit the data you import, making tax preparation easier and more accurate.

Many people think tax preparation is complicated or requires years of experience, but the truth is that the core skills matter more than prior experience.

The Core of BAS Preparation

The fundamental skills you need for BAS (Business Activity Statement) preparation include:

📋
BAS Structure

Understanding the structure of BAS reporting across jurisdictions.

📊
Track Sales & Purchases

Knowing how to track sales, purchases, and GST/VAT accurately.

🔍
Audit & Reconcile

Auditing and reconciling imported data for accuracy before submission.

Once you have these core skills, you can handle BAS for almost any business — and even process tax in different countries, as the main principles remain the same.

Rules Are Easy to Learn

Every company, industry, or country may have small differences in reporting rules. These differences are rule-based, which means they're easy to learn once you understand the core structure.

If you ever feel unsure, a tax agent can assist you with specific questions. But most of the work comes down to understanding the structure, not memorizing every exception.

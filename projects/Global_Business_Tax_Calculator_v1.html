AU · Australian Taxation Office
Australia

Income & Corporate Taxes

Corporate Company Income Tax
Tax = Taxable Income × (25% if Base Rate Entity | 30% if Other)
Employee Benefits Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT)
FBT = (Taxable Value × Gross-up Factor) × 47%

Indirect & Consumption Taxes

Consumption Goods and Services Tax (GST)
Net GST = (Sales × 1/11) − (Purchases × 1/11)
Industry Specific Wine Equalisation Tax (WET)
WET = Wholesale Sales Value × 29%
Luxury Asset Luxury Car Tax (LCT)
LCT = (Total Value − LCT Threshold) × 10/11 × 33%

State & Territory Taxes

Employment Payroll Tax
Tax = (Total Wages − Threshold) × State Rate
Property Land Tax (Simplified)
Land Tax = (Land Value − Threshold) × Rate

Excise & Customs

Specific Goods Fuel, Alcohol & Tobacco Excise
Excise = Quantity × Specific Rate

US · Internal Revenue Service
United States

Federal Taxes

Corporate Federal Corporate Income Tax
Federal Tax = Taxable Income × 21%
Payroll FICA (Social Security & Medicare)
SS = Gross Wages (up to limit) × 6.2%  |  Medicare = Gross Wages × 1.45%
Unemployment FUTA (Federal Unemployment Tax)
FUTA = Wages (up to $7,000) × 0.6%

State & Local Taxes

Income State Corporate Income Tax
State Tax = (Taxable Income × Apportionment %) × State Rate
Business Presence Franchise / Privilege Tax
Tax = Capital Stock (or Net Worth) × Rate
Consumption Sales & Use Tax
Sales Tax = Purchase Price × (State Rate + Local Rate)

Federal Excise Taxes

Excise Specific Industry Levies
Excise = Units × Rate

Provincial Provincial Corporate Tax
Provincial Tax = Taxable Income × Provincial Rate

Sales & Indirect Taxes

VAT / Sales GST, HST, PST & QST Remittance

Environmental Carbon Tax (Fuel Charge)

Employment Levies

Health Employer Health Tax (EHT)
EHT = (Annual Payroll − Exemption) × Rate

UK · His Majesty's Revenue & Customs
United Kingdom

Corporate & Business Income

Corporate Corporation Tax
Tax = Profits × (19% if < £50k | 25% if > £250k)  |  Marginal Relief: 3/200 × (£250k − Profits) for between
Employment National Insurance (Employer NICs)
Employer NICs = (Earnings − Secondary Threshold) × 13.8%
Training Apprenticeship Levy
Levy = (Total Pay Bill − £15,000 Allowance) × 0.5%

Indirect & Environmental

Consumption Value Added Tax (VAT)
VAT Payable = Output Tax (Sales) − Input Tax (Purchases)
Environmental Plastic Packaging Tax (PPT)
Tax = Weight (Tonnes) × £217.85
Energy Climate Change Levy (CCL)
CCL = Energy Used (kWh) × Commodity Rate

Property & Other

Local Business Rates
Rates = Rateable Value × Multiplier
Insurance Insurance Premium Tax (IPT)
IPT = Premium Amount × (12% Standard | 20% Higher Rate)

Overview

I'm excited to share a FP&A Dashboard I built to streamline financial planning, forecasting, and performance analysis. The dashboard combines interactive reporting with dynamic visuals to help analyze budgets, variances, and operational performance more efficiently.

Many assume FP&A dashboards require years of advanced experience, but the real advantage comes from understanding how to structure financial data and present insights clearly.

What Makes an FP&A Dashboard Effective

The key skills for building and using FP&A dashboards successfully include:

🗂️

Structure Planning & Forecasting Data

Organize budgets, forecasts, and actuals clearly — revenue, expenses, headcount, and operational drivers — so every plan is instantly traceable and comparable.

👁️

Visualize Variances & Trends

Build visuals that make budget variances, trend shifts, and performance drivers obvious at a glance — variance charts, waterfall breakdowns, and trend lines.

🔍

Add Interactive Scenario Analysis

Enable live filters for scenario analysis and deeper insights — adjust assumptions, compare periods, and explore what-if scenarios without leaving the dashboard.

Once these core skills are in place, FP&A dashboards can adapt to different business models, forecasting methods, and reporting needs.

Designed for Better Financial Planning

Every business has unique budgets, forecasts, and strategic goals. This dashboard is built to make financial analysis more flexible, interactive, and easier to customize.
